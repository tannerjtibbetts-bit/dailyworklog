Good to know. You don't need a Mac. Codemagic builds the iPhone app online from your project. Apple's websites, App Store Connect and Codemagic all work in your phone's web browser (Android is fine). To test the app before sending it to Apple you need an iPhone for a few minutes; borrow one if you don't have one. Costs: Apple charges US$99 a year (in your own currency).
