# Daily Work Log — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Daily Work Log is an app for logging daily field work: checklists, crew, notes, overtime,
attendance, sea can movements, links, contacts and documents.

## What the app collects
**Nothing.** Daily Work Log has no accounts and no servers. The developer does not collect,
store, sell or share any of your information, and the app has no advertising or tracking.

## Where your information is kept
Everything you enter (projects, logs, crew names and phone numbers, notes, photos, documents,
contacts) is saved only on your own phone, inside the app. It is removed if you delete the app.
If you turn on iPhone backups (iCloud or computer), the app's data is included in your own backup.

## Permissions the app asks for
- **Camera** – to take photos of your work and of documents. Photos stay on your phone.
- **Photos** – to add pictures you already have, and to save photos you take to a
  "Daily Work Log" album on your phone.
- **Microphone and Speech Recognition** – only when you tap the mic to speak instead of typing.
  Speech is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition service.

You can turn any of these off at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

## Sharing
The app only sends something when you choose to: for example when you text a crew member,
email or share a report, or open a web link. These go through your phone's own Messages, Mail,
share sheet or web browser, to the people and places you pick.

## Children
The app is meant for work use by adults and does not knowingly collect anything from children.
