**Promotional text** (170 max):
Log the day's field work in minutes: checklists with photos, crew assignments, overtime sign-ups, attendance, notes and sea can moves, then send a clean report.

**Description:**
Daily Work Log keeps a foreman's whole day in one place and turns it into a clean report.

• Daily checklists – tick off each field or task, add photos, mark delays and who completed it.
• Crew – manage your crew in groups, with phone numbers, trade, level and union local. Text a crew member when you assign them.
• Notify – send a note or photo to the crew, or a whole group at once.
• Overtime – weekend sign-up sheet with Yes / No for each day, hours and confirmed. Confirmed people light up green (Yes) or red (No).
• Attendance – who's here today, with comments.
• Notes – tag notes as Safety concern, Training or Crew concern.
• Sea can movements – log every move with can number, locations and weight.
• Links, contacts and documents – keep web links, company contacts and photos or files of documents handy in every project.
• Reports – export the day as PDF, Word or Excel and email or share it.

Everything stays on your phone. No account and no sign-up needed.
